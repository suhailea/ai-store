Staffroom screens

Each screen is a standalone HTML file — open it directly in a browser, no build step.

  • 01Office (Dashboard)the dashboard: Noor’s brief, KPIs, live activity with a blocked injection, approvals waiting and team status.
  • 02Team BoardKanban across all employees: Assigned, Working, Waiting for you, Done.
  • 03Approvalslist of pending actions and the exact preview with approve / edit / reject.
  • 04Teamthe five AI employees as cards plus the people table.
  • 05Employee Profileinstructions, access badge, memory, budget, performance and work history.
  • 06Task Detail & Run Viewstep-by-step trace, cost and stop / retry.
  • 07Create Task (Modal)the New task modal over the board.
  • 08Hire Employee (Form)step 2 of the hiring flow as a sectioned form.
  • 09Invite Staff & Approval Rule (Forms)the two short settings forms.
  • 10Loading & Empty Statesskeletons, loaders, empty states and toasts.
